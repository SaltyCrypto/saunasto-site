Saunasto Field Notes · United States, 2026

America's sauna culture is borrowed and braided. The Finns brought it to Minnesota and Wisconsin. The Russians brought banya to Brighton Beach and Wicker Park. The Koreans brought jjimjilbang to Koreatown LA. Now a new generation is building social bathhouses (Othership, Bathhouse, World Spa) that take from all three traditions and add their own communal vocabulary.

Our gold list is geographically dispersed because the culture is. Cedar + Stone in Duluth, Othership in Brooklyn, Bathhouse in Williamsburg, Wi Spa in LA, Russian Banya of Dallas, Buckstaff in Hot Springs, the social bathhouses in NYC. Each represents one of the country's parallel sauna traditions, not the whole.
