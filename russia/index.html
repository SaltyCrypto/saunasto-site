Russia.

The banya is a Russian sauna only in the sense that the onsen is a Japanese sauna. It is its own institution. Birch venik, parilka heat, plunge pool, repeat. Our 8 Russian Curator's Picks include the Sanduny tradition (Moscow, Saint Petersburg, Novosibirsk), the public banya network, and a few venues that bridge banya and Western sauna.

Saunasto Field Notes · Russia, 2026

The banya is a high-temperature wet-steam ritual using birch or oak veniks (leaf bundles) to flagellate the body, drive blood to the skin, and exfoliate. It is not the same as a Finnish sauna and the people who do it the most will tell you so. It predates the Christianization of Russia.

Our gold list bridges the historic urban banya (Sanduny, Krasnopresnenskie, Mytninskie) and the village banya tradition. Honey Banya Olgino is the rural-meets-modern entry near Saint Petersburg. SAUNA.SPB.RU is included for the architecture.
