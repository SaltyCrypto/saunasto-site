Terme Libere di Petriolo.

Free public wild thermal baths

Free, open-air wild thermal baths along the Farma river at Bagni di Petriolo. Several stone pools fed by a 43°C sulfurous spring; open 24h, no entry fee. Site has been used since Etruscan and Roman times.
