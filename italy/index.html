№ 001 · IT-CASCAT

Cascate del Mulino

Free public thermal cascade · Saturnia

Free, open-air thermal cascade pouring 37.5°C sulfurous water into a series of travertine pools. One of Italy's most photographed natural hot springs; open 24h, no entry fee. Walking distance from Terme di Saturnia.

Typehot spring
Heatwood-fired
Accesspublic
№ 002 · IT-TERME-

Terme Libere di Petriolo

Free public wild thermal baths

Free, open-air wild thermal baths along the Farma river at Bagni di Petriolo. Several stone pools fed by a 43°C sulfurous spring; open 24h, no entry fee. Site has been used since Etruscan and Roman times.

Typehot spring
Heatwood-fired
Accesspublic
