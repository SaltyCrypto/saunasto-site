Cascate del Mulino.

Free public thermal cascade · Saturnia

Free, open-air thermal cascade pouring 37.5°C sulfurous water into a series of travertine pools. One of Italy's most photographed natural hot springs; open 24h, no entry fee. Walking distance from Terme di Saturnia.
