Austria.

Austrian thermae sit at altitude. The country's gold-tier saunas are mostly Alpine: lakeside thermal villages in Steiermark, ski-resort thermal centers in Tirol, the Hundertwasser thermal village in Bad Blumau. Our 6 Austrian Curator's Picks privilege design and altitude.

Saunasto Field Notes · Austria, 2026

The Austrian thermal tradition shares the German one but trades urbanity for altitude. Aufguss is here. Naked is here. The towel rule is here. What's added: an alpine view from the warm pool, a wooden chalet vocabulary, and a willingness to spend three hours doing nothing but moving between temperatures.

Therme Loipersdorf's Schaffelbad is regarded as the finest sauna landscape in Europe, full stop. Spa Therme Blumau is the Hundertwasser one (curved, golden, biomorphic, exactly what you'd expect). Therme Wien is the urban anchor in Vienna's tenth district.
