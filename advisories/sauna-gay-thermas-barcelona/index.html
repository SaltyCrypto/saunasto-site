Sauna Gay Thermas

Carrer de la Diputació, 46 · Eixample, Barcelona, Spain  ·  Added 31 May 2026  ·  Expires May 2028
Hygiene concerns Service incident Adult venue
Venue representative? Dispute this advisory →

Visitor reports across multiple years and independent accounts describe three distinct concerns at this venue: hygiene issues including reported mold in facility areas; sexual services appearing to operate on premises; and unequal treatment of guests, including instances where some visitors received lockers while others received trash bags under the same entry price. The venue has a 3.3 rating across 488 Google reviews. Owner responses to complaints have been consistent but the underlying patterns have not resolved over the period covered by reports.

Flag breakdown
Hygiene concerns
Visitor reports cite mold in facility areas and inadequate maintenance relative to the €30 entry price. Multiple independent accounts, spanning at least 12 months.
Service incident
Reports describe guests being given trash bags instead of lockers for storage. At least one account describes this occurring selectively — some guests on the same visit received lockers, others did not, with no stated rationale.
Adult venue
Multiple independent reviewers describe individuals inside the venue being paid for sexual services. The venue is listed on Google as a "gay sauna" and operates 24 hours. This flag is informational — not a judgment on the venue category, but context for visitors who may not expect this.
Evidence basis
Source tier
Verified reviews (Google Maps, 488 total reviews, 3.3 aggregate rating)
Independent sources
6 independent accounts identified across the three flag types; each flag type supported by 2+ non-connected reports
Time span
Reports span at least 12 months prior to this notice (earliest: August 2024, most recent: January 2026)
Pattern assessment
Owner has responded to negative reviews with boilerplate. The underlying concerns recur across multiple reporters over multiple periods, indicating the pattern is unresolved rather than isolated.
Official sources
None on file at time of publication. This advisory is based on community reports only.
Language standard
All statements in this notice use attributive framing — "visitor reports describe" and "accounts indicate." This notice documents reported concerns; it does not assert that any specific allegation is proven.
