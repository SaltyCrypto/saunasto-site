"The Romans built Bath. The British are still using it."
Saunasto Field Notes · United Kingdom, 2026

Britain's bathing tradition runs through the Roman thermae at Bath, the Victorian Turkish bath revival, the seaside bathing machines, and now the wood-fired barrel saunas that line the country's beaches. The recent boom is driven by cold-water swimmers, who needed somewhere warm to go after.

Our gold list privileges the historically continuous (Roman Baths of Bath), the seaside wave (Beach Box, Seaside Sauna Haus), the London banya tradition (Banya No.1, The Bath House), and the new community-built saunas (Community Sauna Baths, Skuna). Each represents a different strand of the British revival.
