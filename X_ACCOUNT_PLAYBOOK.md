# @saunasto — X Account Playbook
# Advisory + media engagement templates

---

## Account bio

```
The world's saunas, indexed. Hand-curated. Honestly rated.
Venue advisories at saunasto.com/advisories
```

---

## Template A — Advisory already placed, replying to a news story

When a journalist or account posts about an incident at a venue we've already flagged:

```
We placed a venue advisory on [Venue] in [City] following
community reports of [1-2 word summary]. Sources on file.
saunasto.com/advisories/[slug]
```

Keep it under 2 lines. No opinion, no adjectives. The link does the work.

**Example (Thermas):**
```
We placed a venue advisory on Sauna Gay Thermas in Barcelona
following community reports of hygiene concerns and unequal
guest treatment. Sources on file.
saunasto.com/advisories/sauna-gay-thermas-barcelona
```

---

## Template B — Story breaks, not yet reviewed

When news breaks and we haven't placed an advisory yet:

```
We're aware of reports about [Venue] in [City].
Our editorial team is reviewing the listing — we'll
update within 48 hours. saunasto.com/advisories
```

Never skip the 48h review. Speed is not worth the liability.

---

## Template C — Advisory placed proactively (no news hook)

Post unprompted when a new advisory goes live:

```
New venue advisory: [Venue], [City].
[Flag 1] · [Flag 2]
Sources on file. Appeal process open.
saunasto.com/advisories/[slug]
```

**Example:**
```
New venue advisory: Sauna Gay Thermas, Barcelona.
Hygiene concerns · Service incident · Adult venue
Sources on file. Appeal process open.
saunasto.com/advisories/sauna-gay-thermas-barcelona
```

---

## What NOT to post

- No adjectives about the venue ("disgusting", "horrifying")
- No retweets of graphic claims or unverified allegations  
- Never tag the venue directly — it invites a fight and looks aggressive
- Never post about a venue before the advisory is live on the website
- Never reference the advisory "blacklist" — that word never appears publicly

---

## Content mix (non-advisory posts)

Advisory posts should be ~20% of the feed. Rest:
- Discovery: remarkable venues, unusual bathing cultures
- Regional: "5 thermal baths in [country]" threads
- Behind the scenes: how the index works, data notes
- User highlights (with permission)
- Seasonal: winter plunge culture, summer onsen, etc.

The advisory posts land harder when they're not the only thing you post.
