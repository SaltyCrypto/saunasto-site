About this venue

Ribersborgs Kallbadhus was built in 1898 on a pier extending from the Malmö shoreline into the Öresund. The building sits on the water rather than beside it: the sauna rooms are positioned directly above the sea, and the ladders down to the water are the cold plunge. There is no pool, no controlled temperature, no waiting — just the Öresund, whatever temperature it is that day.

The structure is wooden, white-painted, and unchanged in its essentials since construction. It is open year-round, which means in winter the sea is cold in the serious Scandinavian sense. The Öresund is visible through the windows from the sauna benches. On the Denmark-facing side, you can see Copenhagen. This is what the kallbadhus form was designed to offer: a democratic public sauna with open water beneath it, available to anyone who shows up.
