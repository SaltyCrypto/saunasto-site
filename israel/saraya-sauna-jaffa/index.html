About this venue

Saraya occupies a position on Tayelet Mifraz Shlomo, the promenade that runs along the Jaffa seafront facing the bay of Tel Aviv. The setting is not incidental: Old Jaffa is one of the oldest continuously inhabited ports in the world, and the promenade runs directly alongside it. A 24-hour gay sauna in this location is a particular kind of Tel Aviv statement — the ancient city backdrop treated as scenery for the present.

The venue operates under two names in community listings: SARAYA SAUNA and Saraya Gay Sauna Jaffa, which is the more commonly used form in navigation and review contexts. The Jaffa designation is meaningful — Jaffa has a distinct character from the Tel Aviv centre, denser, older, and more architecturally layered. The sauna sits within that context rather than apart from it.
