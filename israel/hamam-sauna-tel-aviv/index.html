About this venue

Hamam Sauna Tel Aviv is the most established gay sauna on this list, operating on HaRakevet Street in the old railway district of central Tel Aviv. The name is not coincidental: the layout follows hammam principles — a sequence of heat rooms of different intensities, wet and dry options, common areas for socialising between sessions. In a city that tends toward the new, this venue has built a reputation through longevity and consistency.

The HaRakevet district has changed considerably over the past decade — it sits adjacent to the old central station neighbourhood, which has itself been in a long process of transformation. The venue's 4.8 rating across 94 reviews is the kind of score that reflects a stable community rather than a tourism spike. People return. That is the relevant data point.
