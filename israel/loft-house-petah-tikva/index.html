About this venue

Petah Tikva is one of the oldest Jewish cities in modern Israel, sitting about 10 kilometres east of central Tel Aviv. It functions today as a dense suburb and commercial city in its own right, with a population large enough to sustain its own sauna infrastructure independent of the Tel Aviv venues. Loft House serves that population.

The venue name in Hebrew (לופט האוס) and the English transliteration both use "Loft House" — a naming convention that suggests a multi-level layout, with the sauna facilities occupying an upper floor or adapted industrial space. This format is common in Israeli urban saunas, which frequently operate out of converted commercial buildings rather than purpose-built structures. The 4.4 rating across 27 reviews reflects consistent neighbourhood use rather than destination traffic.
