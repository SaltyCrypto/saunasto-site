About this venue

Carlebach Street sits in the centre of Tel Aviv, a block from the old central bus station area and close to several of the city's creative and nightlife districts. Sauna Club occupies this location as a hybrid venue: the sauna function and the nightlife function coexist in a format that is more common in Berlin or Amsterdam than in the Middle East. That context matters. Tel Aviv supports this kind of venue because the city has built the social infrastructure around it.

The venue is newer than Hamam and Saraya on this list, with a small but positive review footprint. The LGTB+ designation in the name is explicit rather than coded, which is the Tel Aviv norm: venues here tend to identify directly rather than rely on community knowledge alone. It is, by the standards of the region, a statement venue operating in a city that has decided to make that statement.
