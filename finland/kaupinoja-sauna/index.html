Kaupinoja Sauna.

The whole family can enjoy Kaupinoja sauna. Kaupinoja sauna is practically open all the time, except on the second Tuesday of every month, when maintenance is carried out there. In our large sauna you will get quality steam, after which it is nice to take a dip in the refreshing lake water. You can swim in Lake Näsijärvi both in summer and in winter. From the sauna, a path and stairs lead directly to the lake, so the passage is smooth. Welcome to the hot steam!
