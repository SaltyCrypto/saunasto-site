Saunasto · 2026

Saunasto means "the saunas, gathered" in Finnish. We chose the name because the project is what the word describes: a working index of the world's bathing-culture venues, sorted by type, country, and quality. The base catalogue inside our iOS app holds 19000 active venues across 35 countries. The Curator's Picks above are the 157 we think every sauna lover should know about.

Selection is editorial, not algorithmic. The criteria: real saunas (not tanning salons or hotel-spa add-ons), real cultural lineage (banya, onsen, jjimjilbang, Finnish, German, Hungarian, hammam), real water (no chlorine baths labelled "thermal"), and a hard preference for venues you can actually visit. Hotel-only saunas score lower than public ones. Membership-only clubs are excluded unless the membership is realistically obtainable.

About

Why this exists.

Search "sauna" on a global map and you get gym lockers, tanning beds, and pool-supply shops mixed in with real venues. We separate them at the data layer.

Saunasto is a working index of 19000 verified saunas across 35 countries, with 157 hand-picked Curator's Picks and editorial coverage of the bathing tradition in every country we list. The iOS app is the primary product; this website is the open reference index. Built by a solo developer plus a curator panel. Updated continuously.
