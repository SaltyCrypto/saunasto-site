Germany.

Germany has the thermal-spa town. The Bäder, the Therme, the Aufguss ritual, the firm rule that the sauna is naked and the towel is for sitting, not covering. Our 8 German Curator's Picks include Friedrichsbad, Therme Erding (Europe's largest), Vabali Spa, and the Hundertwasser-influenced Liquidrom.

From the Field Journal
"Wenn man hineinkommt, ist man Mensch. Beim Aufguss ist man Tier. Beim Hinausgehen ist man wieder Mensch, aber etwas leichter."
Saunasto Field Notes · Germany, 2026

Aufguss is what makes a German sauna a German sauna. The sauna master pours water on the rocks, waves a towel through the rising steam, and circulates concentrated heat through every body in the room. It is theatrical, communal, often choreographed to music, and never optional.

Our gold list privileges Aufguss-strong houses (Therme Erding, Mediterana) and the historically significant (Friedrichsbad, dating to 1877). Vabali Spa earns gold for combining Asian-inspired architecture with German-quality löyly without the kitsch.
